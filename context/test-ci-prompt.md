real network calls in unit tests.

Priority order (highest business risk first — cover these before anything else):
  1. Anything touching money: apps/backend/src/modules/finance,
     apps/backend/src/modules/invoices, apps/backend/src/modules/transactions,

     bookings, guests, students modules.
  3. Auth/authorization: apps/backend/src/core/guards/clerk-auth.guard.ts
     and roles.guard.ts — test allowed/denied paths per role (owner/manager/
     staff) with mocked Clerk sessions.

  5. Remaining modules (bookings, rooms, guests, alerts, notifications, ocr,
     ai-ingestion, isp, academic-terms, school-classes, table-orders, notes) —
